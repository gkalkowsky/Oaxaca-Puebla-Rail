## Verdict

One of: **GO** (screen finds no disqualifier; commission a real study) /
**NO-GO** (a stop rule triggered; the reason is stated below) /
**NEEDS-MORE-WORK** (a decisive question cannot be answered from available
sources; the specific missing evidence is named).

A verdict is written only when it is supported by cited sources. "No evidence
found" is never rendered as "no problem exists".

---

## 1. Question

Can a rail connection between Oaxaca de Juárez and Puebla de Zaragoza plausibly
carry enough traffic, at a plausible cost, to be worth studying properly?

This screen answers *plausibly worth studying*. It does not answer *should be
built*.

## 2. Stop rules

Checked in order. The first that triggers ends the screen — remaining sections
are left unanswered on purpose, the finding is written to
`working/stop-rule-finding.md`, and everything is committed immediately.

| # | Stop rule | Trigger condition | Status |
|---|-----------|-------------------|--------|
| SR-1 | **Terrain** | Sustained ruling grade over the Sierra Madre crossing exceeds what adhesion rail can work at acceptable cost, with no alignment avoiding it inside a plausible corridor length | not evaluated |
| SR-2 | **Demand floor** | Total corridor intercity travel across all modes is below breakeven ridership even at 100% mode capture | not evaluated |
| SR-3 | **Capital order-of-magnitude** | Capital cost per km, benchmarked against comparable mountain rail projects, puts total cost outside any plausible funding envelope by more than an order of magnitude | not evaluated |
| SR-4 | **Legal / institutional bar** | An existing concession, protected area, or statutory obstacle forecloses the corridor with no identified route around it | not evaluated |
| SR-5 | **Evidence floor** | Primary sources for corridor geometry, demand and cost cannot be obtained, leaving no auditable basis for any verdict | not evaluated |

SR-5 yields **NEEDS-MORE-WORK**, not NO-GO. Absence of evidence is a gap in
this screen, not a fact about the project.
